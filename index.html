About NorthPoint

Recruiting Built on
Relationships, Not Résumés

NorthPoint Search Group is a highly reputable full-service recruiting firm dedicated to sourcing and placing top-tier professionals across multiple industries. Since 2005, our dedicated team has made us one of the leading recruitment firms in the Southeast.

We believe in fostering strong partnerships built on trust, integrity, and mutual success. Our approach is deeply rooted in consultation — we take the time to understand the unique requirements and culture of each client before we ever send a résumé.

Based in Atlanta and serving clients across the nation, our specialized divisions cover Accounting & Finance, Legal, Construction, Marketing, Healthcare Finance, Administrative, and HR & Recruiting.

How It Works

From Brief to Hired
in Four Clear Steps

No black boxes. You'll know exactly where your search stands at every stage.

1

Discovery Call

We learn your business, role, culture, and success metrics — going far beyond the job description.

2

Tell Us Your Needs

After the call we have a clear picture of your requirements, timeline, and must-have qualities.

3

Vetted Shortlist

A curated shortlist of 3–5 pre-interviewed, confirmed-interested candidates. We build a screening questionnaire with your input, and every résumé comes with the candidate's answers — your questions answered up front.

4

Close & Onboard

We manage offer negotiation, counteroffers, and stay engaged through the first 90 days to protect your investment.

Example: Screening Questionnaire

Built around one client's system implementation

A client running five different systems wanted to select and implement one new system — positioning the company for its next 10 years of growth. Implementation experience was the key skill for the role, so we built their screening questionnaire around it. Every résumé arrived with the candidate's answers about the system selections and implementations they'd led, so the client could see who had that experience before the first call.

What's Different

We Answer Your Questions
Before You Open the Résumé

Most firms send you a stack of résumés and let you work out the rest. We do the screening you would otherwise have to do yourself.

📝

A screening questionnaire built with you

Before we start, we build a custom set of questions around what actually matters for the role — with your input. Every candidate answers them, and their answers travel with the résumé. You get your questions answered up front instead of spending a first interview on them.

🎥

An optional video introduction

Every candidate we present is invited to record a short video intro. It is entirely optional, so not every résumé will have one — but when it does, you get a read on communication and presence long before you book a call.

🤝

We learn the role before we send anyone

We take the time to understand the requirements, the team and the culture before a single résumé goes out.
