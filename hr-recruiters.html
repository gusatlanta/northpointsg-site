HR & Talent Acquisition Recruiters in Atlanta

NorthPoint Search Group recruits HR and talent acquisition professionals for Atlanta employers, from HR generalists and corporate recruiters to CHROs. As recruiters ourselves, we know what good recruiting work looks like and screen for it directly. NorthPoint runs this search directly for non-finance roles.

Get Started

HR and Recruiting Roles We Fill

CHROs and VPs of HR, HR directors and managers, HR business partners, HR generalists, total rewards and benefits leaders, employee relations specialists, talent acquisition directors and managers, corporate recruiters, and recruiting coordinators.

How We Screen HR Leaders

We ask HR candidates about the size and makeup of the workforce they supported, the systems they ran, and the hardest employee relations matter they handled. For talent acquisition candidates, we ask about hiring volume, time to fill and the sourcing they did personally.

Contract HR and Recruiting Support

For hiring surges, system implementations and leave coverage, we place contract HR generalists and recruiters and act as Employer of Record.

Questions We Ask Before an HR Search

Does the role lean toward employee relations, recruiting, benefits or strategy? How many employees and locations will it support, and in which states? Is there an HR system in place, or will this person choose one? We use the answers to decide whether you need an experienced generalist, a specialist or a senior leader who can build a team.

Where We Recruit HR Talent

Across metro Atlanta, including headquarters in Cumberland and Perimeter Center, growth companies in Alpharetta and multi-site employers in Roswell and Gwinnett, plus remote HR professionals nationwide.
