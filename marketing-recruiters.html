Marketing Recruiters in Atlanta

NorthPoint Search Group recruits marketing professionals and leaders for Atlanta companies, from a single digital marketing specialist to a CMO. We place Direct Hire and contract marketing talent across B2B, consumer, healthcare and professional services employers. NorthPoint runs this search directly for non-finance roles.

Marketing Roles We Fill

Chief Marketing Officers and VPs of Marketing, marketing directors and managers, demand generation and growth leaders, product marketing managers, brand and content managers, SEO and paid media specialists, marketing operations and automation leads, and communications managers.

How We Screen Marketers

Marketing résumés tend to claim credit broadly. We ask candidates which results they owned, what budget they managed, which channels they ran themselves and which they managed through an agency. Those answers come with every résumé we send, alongside the screening questions we build with you.

Contract Marketing Help

For launches, rebrands and leaves of absence, we place contract marketers and handle payroll as Employer of Record, so you can add capacity without adding headcount.

Questions We Ask Before a Marketing Search

Is this role mainly about brand, about pipeline, or about running the marketing stack? Will the hire manage an agency or replace one? Does the person need to write and design, or direct others who do? Those answers decide whether you need a generalist marketing manager, a demand generation specialist or a senior leader, and they often change the title before we post anything.

Where We Recruit Marketing Talent

Across metro Atlanta, including Midtown's agency community, the Alpharetta tech corridor and headquarters at Perimeter and Cumberland, plus remote marketers nationwide.
