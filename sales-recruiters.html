Sales Recruiters in Atlanta

NorthPoint Search Group recruits sales leaders and sellers for Atlanta companies: revenue executives who build teams, account executives who close, and the business development and sales operations people who keep pipeline moving. NorthPoint runs this search directly for non-finance roles.

Get Started

Sales Roles We Fill

Chief Revenue Officers and VPs of Sales, regional and national sales directors, enterprise and mid-market account executives, business development managers, channel and partner managers, inside sales and sales development leaders, customer success leaders, and sales operations managers.

How We Screen Sellers

We ask for quota, attainment and deal size in writing, then check them through references. We also ask how a candidate sourced their own pipeline and how long their sales cycles ran, because a seller who thrived in short transactional deals may struggle in a nine-month enterprise cycle.

Building a Team, Not Just Filling a Seat

When you are hiring several sellers at once, we can recruit the sales leader first and then the team, so the new leader helps choose their own people.

Questions We Ask Before a Sales Search

Will this person hunt for new logos or grow existing accounts? How long is your typical sales cycle, and who signs the contract on the buyer side? How is the role paid, and how quickly can a new hire realistically earn commission? Clear answers help us find sellers whose past success happened in conditions like yours, which is the best predictor we know of.

Where We Recruit Sales Talent

Across metro Atlanta, including software and fintech in Alpharetta, distribution and manufacturing in Gwinnett, franchise systems at Perimeter and headquarters in Cumberland, plus remote sellers nationwide.

Sales Recruiting FAQ

Do you recruit CROs and VPs of Sales?

Yes. We run Direct Hire and retained searches for revenue leaders.

How do you verify a seller's numbers?

We ask for quota and attainment in writing and confirm them through references.

Can you hire a sales leader and then their team?

Yes. We can sequence the searches so the leader takes part in choosing the team.

Do you recruit remote account executives?

Yes. We recruit remote sellers across the U.S.
