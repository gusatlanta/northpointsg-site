Executive Search Firm in Athens, GA

Athens combines regional employers with a steady flow of marketing, business and communications talent from the University of Georgia. NorthPoint Search Group recruits marketing, sales and HR leaders for Athens companies and helps Atlanta employers reach Athens-based leaders for hybrid and remote roles. NorthPoint runs this search directly for non-finance roles.

Regional Employers and a University Talent Pipeline

UGA's business and journalism schools, including the Terry College of Business and the Grady College, feed the region with marketing, sales and communications professionals. Some stay in Athens and grow into leadership roles there; others are recruited away to Atlanta.

Athens employers in healthcare, manufacturing and hospitality hire HR and marketing leaders who want a smaller-city pace. Atlanta companies hire Athens leaders when the role can run hybrid along the GA-316 corridor or fully remote.

Leadership Roles We Recruit in Athens

Marketing directors and communications leaders, regional sales managers, HR managers and directors for healthcare and manufacturing employers, and remote marketing and sales leaders for Atlanta companies. See our marketing, sales and HR and recruiting practices.

Selling Athens to Senior Candidates

Leaders relocating from larger cities often weigh cost of living, schools and the university town feel. We cover those questions early so finalists arrive ready to say yes.

How a NorthPoint Search Works

After a discovery call, we build screening questions with you and send a short list of interviewed, interested candidates whose answers come with each résumé. Each candidate is invited to record an optional video intro.

For accounting and finance roles in Athens, Staff Financial Group, NorthPoint's accounting and finance brand, leads the search, including CFO and controller.
