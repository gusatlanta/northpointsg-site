Executive Search Firm in Duluth, GA

Duluth is a U.S. base for subsidiaries of overseas companies and for distributors serving the Southeast. NorthPoint Search Group recruits the sales, marketing and HR leaders who connect those businesses to American customers and employees. NorthPoint runs this search directly for non-finance roles.

Leading U.S. Operations for Global Parents

A U.S. subsidiary often needs a sales leader who can win American accounts while reporting to a headquarters abroad, sometimes in a second language. Korean-English bilingual leadership is a frequent request in Duluth, and we can make it a requirement.

The same companies need HR leaders who understand U.S. employment law, benefits and hiring norms, and who can explain them clearly to a parent company used to different rules.

Leadership Roles We Recruit in Duluth

U.S. sales directors and VPs for international subsidiaries, bilingual business development leaders, HR directors for foreign-owned employers, marketing leaders entering the U.S. market, and sales leaders for distributors along I-85. See our marketing, sales and HR and recruiting practices.

Cross-Border Expectations

We confirm travel to the parent company, reporting lines and decision rights with candidates early, because those details decide whether a leader succeeds in a subsidiary role.

How a NorthPoint Search Works

After a discovery call, we build screening questions with you and send a short list of interviewed, interested candidates whose answers come with each résumé. Each candidate is invited to record an optional video intro.

For accounting and finance roles in Duluth, Staff Financial Group, NorthPoint's accounting and finance brand, leads the search, including CFO and controller.
