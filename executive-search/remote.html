Remote Executive Search

Distributed companies hire leaders wherever the best person lives. NorthPoint Search Group recruits remote sales, marketing and HR leaders nationwide, with recruiters who work markets across the Southeast, Texas, the Midwest and California. NorthPoint runs this search directly for non-finance roles.

Get Started

When a Leader Can Lead Remotely

Remote leadership works when goals are measurable and the team already runs on written communication: a VP of Sales with clear pipeline targets, a demand generation leader with channel metrics, or an HR leader supporting a distributed workforce.

Roles that depend on daily in-person culture building or on-site operations are harder to run remotely, and we will say so before a search starts.

Remote Leadership Roles We Recruit

Remote VPs of Sales and regional sales directors, demand generation and content marketing leaders, remote HR and people operations leaders, and talent acquisition leaders for distributed hiring. See our marketing, sales and HR and recruiting practices.

Interviewing Leaders You Haven't Met

Every candidate is invited to record an optional video intro, so you can get a sense of how they communicate before the first call. We also schedule final-round meetings in person when the role calls for it.

How a NorthPoint Search Works

After a discovery call, we build screening questions with you and send a short list of interviewed, interested candidates whose answers come with each résumé. Each candidate is invited to record an optional video intro.

For remote accounting and finance roles, Staff Financial Group, NorthPoint's accounting and finance brand, leads the search, including CFO and controller.

Remote Executive Search FAQ

Do you recruit remote leaders outside Georgia?

Which leadership roles work well remotely?

Sales, marketing and HR roles with measurable goals and written processes tend to work well.

How do I start a remote search?

Use the Get Started form and note that the role is remote.
