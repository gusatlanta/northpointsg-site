Executive Search Firm in Buckhead, Atlanta

Buckhead is where many Atlanta private equity firms, wealth managers and growth-stage headquarters make leadership decisions. Portfolio Company and Wealth Firm Leadership

Private equity sponsors on Peachtree Road often need a new commercial leader within months of closing a deal: a CRO to professionalize sales, a CMO to reposition the brand, or a CHRO to merge the cultures of an add-on acquisition. These searches have deadlines tied to the investment plan.

Wealth management and advisory firms around Lenox and Phipps hire business development leaders and client-experience heads, where trust and discretion count as much as a track record.

Leadership Roles We Recruit in Buckhead

Chief Revenue Officers and VPs of Sales for portfolio companies, CMOs and brand leaders, CHROs and HR leaders for integrations, business development directors for wealth and advisory firms, and marketing leaders for hospitality and luxury retail. See our marketing, sales and HR and recruiting practices.

Discretion as Standard Practice

Many Buckhead searches replace someone who is still in the seat. We run those searches under a code name, share a blinded profile first, and reveal your company only when a candidate is ready to meet.

How a NorthPoint Search Works

After a discovery call, we build screening questions with you and send a short list of interviewed, interested candidates whose answers come with each résumé. Each candidate is invited to record an optional video intro.

For accounting and finance roles in Buckhead, Staff Financial Group, NorthPoint's accounting and finance brand, leads the search, including CFO and controller.

Buckhead Executive Search FAQ

Do you recruit for private equity portfolio companies?

Yes. We work to the deal's timeline and look for leaders who have worked under sponsor ownership.

Can a search run without naming our company?

Yes. Confidential searches use a code name until a candidate is ready to meet you.

Do you recruit HR leaders for post-acquisition integration?

Yes. We screen for experience combining teams, policies and compensation after a deal.

Who handles CFO searches for Buckhead firms?

Staff Financial Group, our finance and accounting brand.
