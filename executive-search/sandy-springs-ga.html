Franchise Systems and Headquarters at Perimeter

Franchise brands headquartered near the GA-400 and I-285 interchange hire franchise development leaders who sell territories to investors, and field marketing leaders who support hundreds of independent owners. Both roles are specialized, and we screen for real franchise experience.

Healthcare organizations in the Medical Center area and national headquarters nearby hire HR leaders who can manage large, diverse workforces and complex benefits programs.

Leadership Roles We Recruit in Sandy Springs

Franchise development directors and VPs, field and brand marketing leaders for multi-unit systems, CHROs and HR directors for large employers, total rewards leaders, and enterprise sales directors for insurance and services companies. See our marketing, sales and HR and recruiting practices.

Commuting to Perimeter

The Red Line stations at Medical Center, Dunwoody, Sandy Springs and North Springs bring leaders in from intown neighborhoods, while GA-400 serves North Fulton. Many Perimeter employers offer hybrid schedules to offset traffic at the interchange.

How a NorthPoint Search Works

After a discovery call, we build screening questions with you and send a short list of interviewed, interested candidates whose answers come with each résumé. Each candidate is invited to record an optional video intro.

For accounting and finance roles in Sandy Springs, Staff Financial Group, NorthPoint's accounting and finance brand, leads the search, including CFO and controller.
