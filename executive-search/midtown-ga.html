Executive Search Firm in Midtown Atlanta

Midtown's mix of Tech Square startups, creative and digital agencies and big headquarters along Peachtree Street creates leadership searches of every size. NorthPoint Search Group recruits first sales leaders, heads of marketing and heads of people for Midtown employers. NorthPoint runs this search directly for non-finance roles.

First Leaders and Senior Specialists in Midtown

A startup near Georgia Tech hiring its first VP of Sales needs someone who will still carry a bag, write the playbook and hire the first reps. That is a different person from the sales executive a large company wants, and we recruit for each separately.

Agencies and media companies in Midtown hire creative directors, account leaders and growth marketers whose portfolios matter more than their titles. We review the work, not just the résumé.

Leadership Roles We Recruit in Midtown

First VPs of Sales and founding account executives, heads of marketing and growth, agency account and creative leaders, heads of people and first HR hires, and talent acquisition leads for companies hiring fast. See our marketing, sales and HR and recruiting practices.

Transit and the Midtown Candidate Pool

Rail access at the North Avenue, Midtown and Arts Center stations widens the pool to leaders who prefer not to drive. Many Midtown leaders also expect a hybrid schedule, so we confirm both before you meet anyone.

How a NorthPoint Search Works

After a discovery call, we build screening questions with you and send a short list of interviewed, interested candidates whose answers come with each résumé. Each candidate is invited to record an optional video intro.

For accounting and finance roles in Midtown, Staff Financial Group, NorthPoint's accounting and finance brand, leads the search, including CFO and controller.
